Hatchabot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Hatchabot is a self-hosted personal assistant platform. Each installation is operated privately by the individual or household that runs it on their own hardware. This policy describes how a Hatchabot installation handles data, including data accessed through Google APIs.

Who operates this installation

This installation is run by its owner on their own server. The operator — not Hatchabot as a product — is the party you connect your accounts to. Questions about this specific installation go to its operator.

What Google data we access, and why

Only when you explicitly connect a Google account, and only for the services you approve during Google's consent screen, a Hatchabot agent may access:

  • Gmail — to read and, where you enable it, send email on your behalf, so an assistant can triage your inbox or send messages you ask it to.
  • Google Calendar — to read and manage events you ask it to schedule.
  • Google Drive / Sheets / Docs — to read or edit documents you point it at.

Access is used solely to perform the tasks you direct your agent to do. It is never used for advertising, and your Google data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services — Limited Use. Hatchabot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the assistant features you request, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, is not used for advertising, and no human reads it except with your explicit consent or for security/legal purposes.

Where your data lives

Hatchabot is self-hosted: your data and the OAuth tokens for your connected accounts are stored on the operator's own server, not on any Hatchabot-operated cloud. Refresh tokens are held in an encrypted secret store on that server and are used only to obtain short-lived access tokens when your agent acts. Agents run in isolated containers on the same server.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time:

  • Disconnect the account in Hatchabot (Settings → Connections). This deletes the stored token, removes it from every agent, and revokes it with Google.
  • Or revoke Hatchabot's access directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Once revoked, the installation can no longer access that account's Google data.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the software changes; the date above reflects the current version.
